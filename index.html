ABOUT THE PROJECT

Acadense

To-do List System in Academic Workflows of Grade 12 Students

What is Acadense?

Acadense is a time management and to-do list system designed to help Grade 12 students organize their academic workflows. It brings tasks, schedules, notes, and time-management tools together in one focused workspace.

Developed for

Integrated Innovation and Hospitality Colleges, La Forteza

The idea behind Acadense

Academic work can become difficult to manage when tasks, deadlines, and personal routines compete for attention. Acadense was created to provide a simpler space where students can plan what matters, keep track of their progress, and work at their own pace.

Created by

Grade 12 Information and Communications Technology (ICT) Students of Integrated Innovation and Hospitality Colleges, La Forteza.
